      Good to know

      A few questions.

      What people ask before they make one.

      Do I have to write the lyrics?

      No. You give us names, moments and what you want to say. We write the lyrics from that.

      Is the song made with AI?

      Yes. We use AI music tools to turn your story into lyrics and a full production. You hear the result and can ask for changes before you give it.

      Can I choose the sound and the language?

      Yes. Pick a style in the order form, or tell us an artist or mood you have in mind. Tell us the language too. The samples above are in English and Spanish.

      What if a name or a line is wrong?

      Every package includes rounds of changes: one in Essential, two in Signature, three in Deluxe.

      How do I get the song?

      We send it to you by reply to your order, with everything else in your package. You decide when and how they hear it.

      How do I pay?

      Nothing is charged on this page. After you send your order we reply with the total and how to pay, and we start once that’s done.

      Can I use the song for my business?

      Gift songs are for personal use: playing, sharing and posting them as a gift. For ads, shops and podcasts, choose the Business jingle, which comes with a written license.
